Test the drain's end against a performed send queued in the ready list

Found during item c5ca75. `Conn.cut` (zig/src/serve.zig, `.send, .wrote => self.expire()`) handles a performed send (`.wrote`) still waiting in the loop's ready list when the shutdown's drain ends: `expire` sets `closing`, and the ready turn then delivers the result to the close, unreported. No test covers that branch. In zig/src/serve_test.zig the drain is ended only by the test of a send waiting in the kernel (`s.lp.loop.setTimer(&s.lp.drain, ...)` at the line after "The send goes on after the stop, under the drain", about line 1180) and by `Single.deinit`'s cleanup (about line 605). Item c5ca75's test stops before the drain ends: its ready turn runs first and the result is reported.

Proposed: one explicit-order test with qgrnr3's `hold`/`forward` helpers. Hold the loop's `wake` and the drain timer's completion, open a connection whose response is performed at once (`respond_on_open`, `report_sent`), run the stop, make the drain due, forward the drain's expiry before the wake, then the wake. Assert that the send is not reported (the log is open, free), the connection closes exactly once, and `finish` ends with no drain armed. Also state the disposition next to `LoopCtx.stopCb`'s comment (which item c5ca75 wrote) if the test shows anything different. No transport behavior change.
