Accept every pending connection per loop turn, so lsrv-arena serves all of HttpArena's 4,096 connections and loses its over-5 s tail

Found by item 6p6tu6 (README, "The over-5 s tail: connections accepted late").

What happens: `LoopCtx.acceptCb` (zig/src/serve.zig) opens the one connection its accept returned and re-adds the accept. zio 53ed3c377317 tries it at once but queues the completion, and `processCompletions` runs only what was queued at entry (src/ev/loop.zig, "Drain only what was queued at entry ... The rest runs next tick"). So each of the two loops accepts one connection per turn, and a turn under HttpArena's load serves hundreds of connections. Locally (bench/arena/local.sh, two rounds): only 1,944-2,257 of 4,096 connections were accepted during the 5 s load, the slowest after 5.1 s; 10-16 (baseline) and 142-181 (pipelined) responses over 5 s. gcannon counts only answered requests, so its throughput, latency and CPU-per-response figures for lsrv-arena cover only the accepted half; the VPS figures very likely have the same flaw (not measured there).

Checked cause: a diagnostic build in which acceptCb takes up to 64 more pending connections with `accept(2)` (non-blocking listener; EAGAIN ends it) before re-adding the accept: 4,096/4,096 accepted during the load, slowest 0.25-0.39 s, 0 responses over 5 s in all four samples, p99.9 0.19-0.22 s baseline and 0.69 s pipelined. The median rose (35 ms, 390-410 ms) since twice the connections share the CPUs.

Options:
1. In acceptCb, loop `net.accept` until EAGAIN, the slot limit (`reserveSlot`) or a bound per callback (e.g. 64, so one loop cannot starve its served connections), then re-add the accept. Small, local to serve.zig, same on epoll and io_uring. Recommended.
2. Ask zio upstream for a multishot or "drain until EAGAIN" accept. Larger, outside our tree, and the one-per-tick drain is documented behaviour, not a bug.
3. Keep it and document that the arena figures cover only accepted connections. Leaves the tail and wrong figures.

Changes failure behaviour under overload (more connections admitted per turn, bounded by --max-conns as now), so it is non-trivial; no contract or boundary change. Verification: bench/arena/local.sh (expect accepted 4096/4096, over5s 0), a focused test that N connections queued before one loop turn are all accepted in that turn, ./bench.sh, then a VPS re-run of the arena profiles. The VPS harness (inner.sh) has no accept trace; trace.bt's syscall ids are arm64 and would need x86_64 ones there.

Decided 2026-11-17 by the coordinating session with sol (consult call 36443c1b) under the user's delegation: approved, first. Make at most 64 accepts per callback, counting the completion that started it, and stop earlier on EAGAIN or when the slots run out; re-add the accept once. Reserve a slot before each extra accept and release it if the accept fails. When the slots run out, suspend admission and resume it on the event of a slot's release, with no spinning. Keep the work bounded, so established connections and the other loop sharing the listener still get their turn, without assuming an even split. Tests: hold the callback's dispatch until the clients' handshakes have completed (a positive event), then release it. Assert that a batch within the cap is accepted in that one dispatch, that a larger batch makes progress across dispatches while established connections progress too, and cover both loops, EAGAIN, running out of slots and their release, and cleanup after an error. Rerun bench/arena/local.sh, counting accepted connections. In the same item, mark README's VPS comparisons ('HttpArena on the VPS') invalid: they counted only accepted connections. Separate the cause verified locally from the impact on the VPS, which was not measured. No VPS rerun without the user's new consent (a separate question).
